# AlpyX Global Command 0.50 Alpha — Living World

The first 0.50 slice makes non-player countries persistent strategic actors instead of static data cards.

## Strategic AI
AI governments review their position every two weeks and choose among stabilization, growth, rearmament, resource security and influence expansion. Goals adjust cabinet posture, tax posture and readiness.

## Intelligence
Time-based deterministic operations:
- Gather Intelligence
- Influence Campaign
- Sabotage Industry
- Counter Network

Success and compromise risk are derived from attacker intelligence capacity versus target counter-intelligence and stability.

## Influence
Relations, shared ideology, shared religion and bloc membership now feed a soft-power influence layer.
