# Starting diplomacy — 0.90.0-playtest.7 (Codex)

Reviewed 2026-10-11. This is an **offline contemporary scenario**, not a live feed or a historical reconstruction of the game calendar's 2026-01-01. Sources establish memberships/agreements/cooperation or tensions; numeric scores are authored game estimates, not official diplomatic ratings. State statements are evidence of that state's position, not an endorsement of territorial claims.

The snapshot is applied only when building initial countries. Existing saves and migrations retain their actual relations. No real-world active wars, occupations, global sanctions, trade flows or membership in the game's fictional institutions are created. Unknown bilateral pairs retain the engine's neutral fallback.

## Interpretation

- NATO: 32 formal military allies; game score 70 and alliance treaty. Uniform group scores simplify individual political disagreements.
- EU: 27 members, score 45 and game trade treaty as a common-market abstraction; military alliance is not inferred from EU membership.
- OTS (5), ASEAN (11, includes Timor-Leste), GCC (6), Mercosur (5, includes Bolivia, excludes suspended Venezuela), SADC (16), EAC (8), CARICOM (14 represented full members): regional-cooperation estimates only, **no automatic military alliance or trade treaty**. OTS observers and CARICOM associate members/absent Montserrat are excluded.
- Türkiye–Azerbaijan: score 90 / alliance, from the Shusha Declaration. USA–Japan/Korea/Australia: defence alliances; Korea/Australia also have existing free-trade agreements. China–Russia/Pakistan: friendly cooperation, no fabricated defence treaty.
- Russia–Ukraine, China–Taiwan, DRC–Rwanda, USA–Iran, Israel–Iran/Palestine: hostile game relation estimates from documented tensions/conflicts. DRC–Rwanda overrides regional cooperation. This does not create active in-game wars.
- Türkiye–Bulgaria retains the established introductory trade route alongside NATO alliance; its trade pact is a gameplay abstraction.

## Coverage and limits

203 represented countries; 109 have authored connections and 932 bilateral pairs are seeded. Every remaining pair is neutral fallback, **not independently researched neutrality**. Broad bilateral research, Pacific and further African/Latin American/South Asian coverage remain follow-ups. The table below discloses coverage for every represented country.
