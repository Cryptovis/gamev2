# 0.99.2 — Slice 8.3 Global Finance & Monetary Programs

Development release on `dev/ron-phase7`, based on v0.99.1 commit `68790ad77bca2a2cddecc5f783804e18bb9d16d9`. Schema 18 retained.

## Delivered behavior

- Scenario capital reservoirs: IMF $250B, World Bank $120B, BRICS+/NDB $50B, OIC/IDB $30B. Each has a distinct `financeBoard`; an OIC/BRICS political vote does not authorize lending. IMF/World Bank board electorates snapshot explicit integer quota units; NDB/IDB boards use member majority with quorum. These are authored RON scenario rules, not current legal institutional quotas or member registers.
- Dormant IMF/World Bank rosters become finance members. A member borrower may request its own financing from the board without holding a board seat. Other countries cannot impose a loan or austerity program on that borrower. Proposals pay 5 influence through the same validator for human and explicit AI actors. Existing political-body access, ballots and histories stay intact.
- Approval rechecks membership, default status, fund capacity, safe deadlines and financial terms. Competing approvals are ordered by effective day and resolution ID; an approval that no longer fits the pool fails explicitly without creating money or debt.
- Adoption reserves the entire principal (`available -= principal`, `committed += principal`) and immediately disburses the initial tranche (`committed -= tranche`, borrower treasury increases). Loans increase principal outstanding and national debt only as cash is drawn. Grants consume capital without creating debt or interest; IMF grants are refused.
- Optional finance payload fields: `initialTrancheB`, `fiscalAdjustmentB` (annual $B, IMF only), `fundingKind: loan | grant`. Defaults are a loan, half initially if conditions exist, otherwise the whole principal. Remaining capital is split over unique sorted condition review days, with the final tranche carrying any rounding remainder. Review dates are absolute campaign days after adoption and before maturity.
- Reviews check all conditions due by the review day: fiscal balance >= target in $B/day, tax rate >= target in percentage points, social-ministry budget share <= target on a 0–1 scale. The existing economy has no distinct subsidy ledger, so `subsidyShare` explicitly uses the social-ministry allocation fraction. Fiscal balance uses the same engine fiscal accounts as actual daily simulation. Failed reviews pause disbursement and cost 3 standing once per pause; reforms can resume the pending tranche on a later day.
- Contract APR uses the existing percent convention: **3.65 means 3.65%**. Daily simple interest is `outstandingB * (apr / 100) / 365`; accrued interest is booked once to national debt, without interest-on-interest. Both organization principal and accrued interest are excluded from generic economy interest. These records never create a duplicate bilateral `sovereignCredit` loan.
- Interest is serviced every 30 days after the initial grace window (`max(30, graceDays)`); all remaining principal and interest are due at maturity. Automatic service transfers only available cash. Voluntary `organization/repayOrganizationFinancing` specifies `financingId`, `principalB`, `interestB`; only the borrower may pay, and treasury, principal and accrued-interest limits are checked. Payments reduce national debt and credit the institution's available funds. Replayed command receipts cannot pay twice.
- `organization/cancelOrganizationFinancing` releases undrawn commitments. Maturity releases expired commitments. Cancellation/expiry never forgives drawn debt: interest and repayment obligations continue while liabilities remain. Grants with expired unused capital retain an `expired` record; fully disbursed grants close.
- IMF fiscal adjustment reduces real daily public spending by annual adjustment / 365, with a floor at zero and aggregate adjustment capped at 20% GDP. Active/paused programs impose proportional daily approval/stability costs. Each tranche provides bounded immediate stability relief and improves the existing liquidity-based solvency assessment while preserving the loan liability. Existing sovereign-credit settlement can use incoming liquidity to settle maturing claims and reduce arrears/default pressure through conserved payments to the creditor; arrears are never deleted for free.
- Missed scheduled service, sovereign arrears or borrower annexation default a live contract and release unused commitments. Default costs 10 organization standing and 5 reputation once per contract. Outstanding defaults/arrears block new loan and grant requests across the four financial institutions. Repayment can cure the financial block; it does not restore reputation or cancelled commitments.

## Tick and persistence boundary

`processOrganizations` has decision and finance stages. Votes close and adopted decisions activate at day start. The country economy excludes organization liabilities from generic interest; its adjustments use a single per-country accounting index. After existing sovereign-credit settlement, the finance stage accrues interest, detects same-day arrears, services due debt, checks tranches and applies domestic costs. Direct calls process both stages; per-contract and organization day markers prevent replay.

`financeVersion?: 1` and `lifecycle?` are additive optional Schema 18 fields. Loading an earlier Schema 18 campaign initializes finance boards and untouched zero pools once, without RNG or input mutation. Existing positive/committed pools and pre-8.3 placeholder financing records are preserved. Placeholder records without lifecycle metadata remain inert rather than guessing their historical disbursements or booking debt twice. Saving/reloading a spent pool cannot replenish it. Existing Schema 12–17 fixtures still migrate to 18.

Strict financing validation checks finite amounts, principal/repayment/tranche identities, commitment coverage, source resolution, dates, immutable loan terms and conditional schedules. The organization effect-reference validator accepts the source-linked financing record for an applied finance resolution. New finance payload fields/statuses need v0.99.2; downgrade loading into older strict validators is not promised.

Amounts are accounted in $B even when terms carry an SDR denomination label. Live SDR conversion/allocation, dedicated subsidy policy, sovereign debt restructuring, legal charter datasets, autonomous finance proposal agendas and the new International Affairs program UI remain later work. Existing scoped sanction records still do not themselves enforce financial-channel sanctions.
